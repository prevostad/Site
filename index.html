About

I am a PhD student in the Scool team at Inria Lille and Université de Lille, advised by Odalric-Ambrym Maillard and Timothée Mathieu. I work on stochastic multi-armed bandits and problem-dependent guarantees: lower bounds and asymptotically optimal algorithms, transfer learning and batched bandits.
